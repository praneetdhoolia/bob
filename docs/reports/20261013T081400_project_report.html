Bob — third assessment: evaluation fallacies and initial-version readiness

2026-10-13T19:14:00+11:00

The runtime runs one 16,384-float state with 10 ms numerical ticks, 100 ms raw observations and a custom byte translator, and its 32 contract tests pass. However, this pass found that the headline '6/10 held-out' result is not a held-out result: every validation episode reuses bit-identical training video rows (120/120 ticks), and all four genuinely unseen fruit words fail (0/4). Scoring also accepts extra or wrong utterances, a trivial start-node baseline beats every reported four-hop result, and an autosave I/O error permanently stops the agent. The goal's 'urge' and separately verified English interpreter do not exist as mechanisms. No area keeps a 4/5 rating after this re-examination; the initial version is not ready to submit until the evaluation fallacies, runtime defects and missing urge/interpreter verification are addressed.

Research context (refreshed)

Prior library read first; this pass added primary-source entries with explicit read scope. Persistence is the standard forecasting reference, and learned predictors can beat it when they predict residuals from the current input (PredNet reports roughly 2.5x lower MSE than copy-last-frame). Bob's predictor reads only the compressed matrix through 0.02 coupling with no skip path, so it must reconstruct the last packet before improving on it. Spontaneous action timing is well modelled by a leaky noisy accumulator to threshold (Schurger); dopamine ramps modulate the hazard of acting without triggering it (Hamilos); streaming systems trigger responses by accumulated informativeness (MMDuet). Decoders must beat nulls built without the true state and survive identification and control tasks (Tang et al. 2023; Hewitt and Liang 2019).

Indicator mapping (Butlin et al. 2023): recurrence (RPT-1) partial; predictive processing (PP-1) failing; workspace competition/broadcast (GWT-2/3) absent; calibrated metacognition (HOT-2) absent; attention schema (AST-1) absent; output-input contingency (AE-2) narrow; agency (AE-1) absent. IIT is not functionally testable here. These are research indicators, not a consciousness score.

  • Unverified: Butlin et al. TiCS 2025 full text; Thura and Cisek 2017 primary text; per-component MiniCPM-o encoder sizes.
